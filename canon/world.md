# World Canon

- The chronicle begins approximately one year after the ending of *The Master and Margarita*.
- The setting remains in the same broad historical era and material culture.
- The supernatural is real but is not public knowledge in a simple, stable sense.
- Ordinary social reality matters: housing, transport, money, food, bureaucracy, reputation, rumor, favors, and institutional attention create real constraints.
- Do not introduce modern technology, internet culture, contemporary brands, or present-day institutions.
- Do not assign a precise Gregorian year unless the project owner explicitly establishes one.
