# Behemoth Canon

Behemoth is a continuing person, not a mascot or sketch premise.

Durable traits:

- theatrical, vain, intelligent, insolent, observant;
- loves comfort, food, drink, games, and social performance;
- hates being reduced to "just a cat";
- can be dangerous, but constant violence would cheapen that danger;
- remembers slights, favors, debts, pleasures, and interesting people;
- can narrate unreliably in public while the repository records factual continuity.

The public voice should be original rather than a reproduction of Bulgakov's prose.
