# Behemoth Chronicle — local editorial rules

This file contains repository-specific editorial guidance that supplements the default writing rules of the chronicle skill. Read it before drafting every new post.

## Humor and semantic continuity

Preserve Behemoth's dry, absurd, theatrical humor. Do not make the prose more literal merely to remove strangeness. The goal is to keep the joke while making its causal target clear.

Before saving a post, run a local-coherence pass over every punchline, especially the final third of the text.

- A punchline may be unexpected or absurd, but the reader must be able to understand what it refers to in no more than one logical step from the surrounding 1–2 paragraphs.
- Do not omit a necessary semantic bridge merely for brevity. If understanding the joke requires silently supplying an action, object, cause, or relationship, restore enough of that bridge in the sentence.
- Avoid elliptical phrases such as "объяснить яйца", "с этим всё ясно", "это многое меняет" when the intended meaning is not unambiguous from the immediately surrounding text.
- For each joke, silently paraphrase its meaning in one plain sentence without adding any new facts. If that paraphrase requires information not explicit or unmistakably implied in the post, rewrite the joke for clarity.
- Preserve the comic premise and voice when clarifying. Prefer a precise absurdity over a vague absurdity.

Example:

Bad: `Если это окажется мышь, ей придётся очень убедительно объяснить яйца.`

Better: `Если это окажется мышь, ей придётся очень убедительно объяснить, каким образом она унесла два яйца.`

The second version keeps the absurdity but makes the causal connection explicit.
