# Fictional World Snapshot

As of 2026-10-11, Behemoth has returned home in good health after visiting the small theatre. He returned Lidia Markovna's marked copy of the play intact, and she stored it in the theatre's prop room. Behemoth no longer owes the book.

Lidia explained that the director requested the cat's presence for the production. The written play contains no cat, and the director's reason is still unknown. Behemoth saw the prop list and objected to the entry "one cat"; Lidia amended it to "by invitation." She knows he can speak; the director and other actors do not.

No further rehearsal, performance or fee has been agreed. Access to the theatre continues through Lidia. Behemoth remains curious about the director's reason.

Blue enamel mug, primus, kerosene supply, household water and kitchen shelf are unchanged and in working order. Chess score: gray-haired player two wins, Behemoth one win, one draw.
