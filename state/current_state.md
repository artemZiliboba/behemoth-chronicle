# Fictional World Snapshot

As of 2026-10-15, Behemoth is well. The small-theatre mystery is resolved: the director uses a cat as an unscripted, neutral witness to make the actors listen to one another. Behemoth has no performance agreement, fee, debt or appointment with the theatre. Lidia Markovna knows he can speak and continues to respect his autonomy; the director and actors do not know this.

The outdoor courtyard chess season has effectively ended because of the cold. The established gray-haired player packed away his set and told Behemoth that he plays on Saturdays in a chess room at a club near a tram stop. No visit or fifth game was scheduled. Chess record remains two wins for the gray-haired player, one for Behemoth, and one draw.

At home, the removable folded newspaper still seals the gap at Behemoth's room window. His blue enamel mug remains clean and intact; the primus works, kerosene and household water are available, and the communal kitchen shelf remains repaired and in use. No immediate obligation requires action.
